Our Goals for
2030

We’re racing to hit these targets to help the world reverse climate change and biodiversity loss. Together, we find the paths to make change possible.

3BL

Restore or prevent pollution of 3 billion liters of wastewater daily through advanced treatment systems—equivalent to stopping nearly all untreated sewage that currently enters the Yamuna.

100M

Protect 100 million citizens living along the Yamuna basin from climate-driven floods and health risks through resilient riverfront planning and green-buffer zones.

650K

Rejuvenate 650,000 hectares of degraded floodplains—reviving natural habitats, wetlands, and grasslands critical for biodiversity and groundwater recharge.

4M

Empower 45 million people across the Yamuna basin—including farmers, urban residents, and local communities—to lead conservation efforts through training, policy participation, and stewardship programs.

1M + 50K

Plant 1 million native trees along the riverbanks—plus 50,000 hectares of riparian forests to stabilize soil, reduce erosion and enhance carbon capture.

4,000 km

Restore 4,000 kilometers of the Yamuna and its tributaries through continuous flow management, pollution control, and ecological revival corridors.
